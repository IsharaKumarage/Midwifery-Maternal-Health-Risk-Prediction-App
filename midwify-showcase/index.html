The Problem

Midwives Need Smarter Tools

Despite Sri Lanka's achievements in maternal health, preventable complications persist — especially in rural and semi-urban settings where specialist access is limited and decisions must be made fast, often with paper records alone.

23%
Reduction in decision time with the app vs. paper-based workflow
18→7%
High-risk case identification error rate dropped in simulation trials

Key Gaps Identified

  • 📱
    No midwife-centered mobile appsAll reviewed ML research systems lack real-time mobile deployment integrated into clinical workflows.
  • 📊
    No interactive data visualizationNo existing system provides live dashboards for monitoring maternal health trends.
  • 🌐
    No trilingual supportSinhala, Tamil, and English support is absent in all reviewed maternal health applications.
The Solution

MidwifeSense AI — What It Does

A Flutter-based cross-platform mobile app that puts a clinical intelligence assistant in every midwife's pocket.

🧠
On-Device ML Risk Prediction
TensorFlow Lite ensemble model (Random Forest + XGBoost) runs directly on the phone, predicting maternal risk in under 2 seconds.
📈
Interactive Health Dashboards
Visualizations of blood pressure, hemoglobin, and fetal heart rate trends across gestational weeks, color-coded by risk zone.
🔔
Voice & Haptic Alerts
Three-tier alert system using voice output in the midwife's chosen language and distinct haptic patterns.
