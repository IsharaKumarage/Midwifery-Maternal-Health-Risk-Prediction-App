The Solution

MidwifeSense AI — What It Does

A Flutter-based cross-platform mobile app that puts a clinical intelligence assistant in every midwife's pocket — from Colombo to the most remote rural health post.

🧠
On-Device ML Risk Prediction
TensorFlow Lite ensemble model (Random Forest + XGBoost) runs directly on the phone, predicting maternal risk in under 2 seconds — even without internet.
📈
Interactive Health Dashboards
Chart.js-powered time-series visualizations of blood pressure, hemoglobin, and fetal heart rate trends across gestational weeks, color-coded by risk zone.
🔔
Voice & Haptic Alerts
Three-tier alert system (normal / warning / critical) using voice output in the midwife's chosen language and distinct haptic patterns — even on silent mode.
🌐
Trilingual Interface
Full Sinhala, Tamil, and English support including clinically accurate terminology translations, validated by field midwives and language experts.
📡
Offline-First Architecture
SQLite caching allows full data entry and risk prediction offline. Automatic Firebase synchronization when connectivity resumes — critical for rural settings.
🔍
Explainable AI (SHAP/LIME)
Every risk prediction is accompanied by a plain-language explanation identifying the most influential vital parameters, building midwife trust and understanding.

Commercialization & Future Potential

From Research to Real-World Impact

MidwifeSense AI is not just an academic prototype. It represents a deployable, scalable mHealth solution with clear pathways into Sri Lanka's public health infrastructure.

Target Markets & Stakeholders

🏥
Ministry of Health & Family Health Bureau
Integration with national antenatal care programs and MOH electronic medical records — reaching thousands of public health midwives island-wide.
🎓
Midwifery Training Institutions
The Learning Support Module makes MidwifeSense AI a clinical training tool for nursing and midwifery schools across Sri Lanka.
🌏
Regional & Global mHealth Expansion
The cultural adaptation framework is replicable across South and Southeast Asian nations with similar community health worker models.
📡
Bluetooth Doppler Device Integration
Future Bluetooth integration with portable Doppler devices would enable automated real-time fetal heart rate monitoring without manual entry.
